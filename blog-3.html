Promoting Digital Literacy and Online Safety for Students

Published by IT Department | The City School Chakwal Campus

As technology becomes central to research, communication, and learning, understanding how to navigate the digital world safely is essential for every student. Digital literacy goes beyond using devices—it involves critical thinking online.

Critical Evaluation of Information

With vast amounts of information available online, identifying credible, fact-checked sources for academic research is crucial. Students are taught to cross-reference facts, verify educational authors, and cite online resources responsibly.

Practicing Cyber Safety

Protecting personal information on public networks, recognizing phishing scams, and maintaining strong passwords are vital safety practices. Keeping personal credentials confidential safeguards online identity and privacy.

Cultivating Positive Digital Footprints

Online actions leave long-lasting traces. Practicing kindness, respecting fellow students in discussion forums, and engaging constructively online help cultivate a positive digital presence beneficial for future academic endeavors.
